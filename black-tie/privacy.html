Privacy policy

Last change: 19 October 2026

In short: Black Tie collects no personal data. It has no account, no ads, and no analytics. The app sends nothing to a server.

Data on your phone

The app keeps your settings on your phone: your player name, your avatar, the number of bots, the pace, the reply timer, and the card names. The app does not send these settings to the internet. To remove them, uninstall the app or clear its storage in the Android settings.

Play with friends on Wi-Fi

When you open or join a table, your phone connects directly to the other phones on the same Wi-Fi network. Your player name and your avatar go to the phones at the table. During a game, the moves go between these phones. No data goes to the internet or to a server. The phones do not save the game after the table closes.

To find tables, the app announces a table on your local network, and it looks for the tables of other phones there. Only devices on the same network can see a table.

Voice chat

Voice chat is off until you turn it on. Then the app uses your microphone, and your voice goes directly to the other phones at the table on your local network. The connection is encrypted (WebRTC). Nobody records or keeps your voice. When you leave the voice chat, the app stops the microphone.
