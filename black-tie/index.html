The game

At the table

Bluff or tell the truth

Claim any role to use its power. Other players can challenge you. A player who lies and gets caught loses a card.

Play against bots

Play against 1 to 5 bots on one phone. Each bot has its own style. Some bluff a lot, and some doubt everything.

Play with friends

One phone opens a table, and the phones on the same Wi-Fi join it. Up to 6 players sit at a table. Bots can fill the empty seats.

Talk at the table

Voice chat goes from phone to phone on your Wi-Fi. The game needs no internet and no server.

Fair clocks

The host sets the pace and the reply timer for all phones. If a player is away for 5 minutes, a bot plays for that player.

Two sets of names

Play with the Black Tie roles, or switch to the classic role names. Each player chooses on their own phone.

Install

Black Tie is not on Google Play yet. You install the APK file from this page.

  1. Open this page on your Android phone, and tap Download for Android.
  2. Open the file that you downloaded. It is in the downloads of your browser, or in the Files app.
  3. If Android asks, let your browser install unknown apps. Then go back.
  4. Tap Install.

Play Protect: Google Play Protect can show a warning, because the app does not come from Google Play. You can let Play Protect scan the app first. Then follow the steps in the warning to install it.

Updates: To update, download the new version from this page and install it over the old one. Your settings stay.

iPhone: An iPhone version is not available yet.

Same Wi-Fi, no internet needed

Play with friends

  1. Connect all phones to the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. One player taps Play with friends, then Open a table. This phone is the host.
  3. The other players tap Play with friends. The table shows up in their list. If it does not show up, they type the address that the host phone shows.
  4. The host adds bots if needed, and taps Deal the cards.

Keep the host app open. The game runs on the host phone. Guest and public Wi-Fi networks often block traffic between phones. If the phones cannot find each other, use a home Wi-Fi network.
